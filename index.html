Common questions

Do I need cloud storage or is an external drive enough?

It depends on how many people need access and from where. An external drive works if one person needs occasional backup, but a team that shares files from different locations usually needs cloud storage too. See the Compare Options page.

How do I know if my business data is backed up properly?

A proper backup has a separate copy, a known location, someone who checks it, and a way to recover files. Run the Backup Readiness Checklist to see where you stand.

What should I look for before choosing new business software?

Cost, setup effort, access, team size, and main use — in that order for most small teams. The Software Comparison Worksheet helps you decide which matters most first.

How can I make an upgrade without replacing everything at once?

Start with the smallest piece that is causing the most trouble. The Technology Upgrade Planning Tool walks through this in stages.

What are the basic cybersecurity steps a small business should not skip?

Unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, and automatic updates cover most of the risk. See the Cybersecurity Basics Checklist.
