About TechBridge

TechBridge is a web-based technology resource hub for small-business owners. It makes sense of common technology decisions without requiring visitors to become experts.

Who it is for

The main audience is small-business owners and managers, usually working with teams of around 5 to 50 people.

What it includes

Articles, checklists, comparison guides, planning tools, templates, and simple explanations about storage, backups, security, business software, remote work, data organization, devices, and planning.

What matters here

Visitors need to be able to use the site on phones and desktops, with a keyboard, screen reader, magnification, voice control, or slower internet. The site should never make someone feel dumb for not knowing a technical term.
